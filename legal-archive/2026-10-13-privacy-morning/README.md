# Personal data policy, first edition of 13 October 2026

Live on 13 October 2026 for a few hours, then replaced the same day by an
edition with the same date once the app changed to match it:

- The Coach now asks for consent before the first question and sends nothing
  without it; it can be withdrawn in Settings → Privacy & AI.
- With "AI Personalization" off only the child's age (adjusted for
  prematurity) goes with a question, no profile details (this edition said the
  profile was always sent).
- Meta receives nothing until tracking is allowed in Apple's prompt (this
  edition said events went to Meta without the advertising identifier), and
  Google's advertising signals (Google Ads) stay off until then too.
